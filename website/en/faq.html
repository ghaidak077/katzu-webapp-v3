Questions before you start

Questions we are actually asked, with short, honest answers. If yours is missing, contact support.

What is Katzu?

Katzu is an AI German-learning app built around speaking, for Arabic speakers from A0 to B2. You pick a real situation from life in Germany, speak or type your sentences, and Katzu corrects you in Arabic.

How do I start? Do I need a card?

Start free from the welcome page: sign-in with a Google account only, no bank card, and you get 3 AI lessons per month that refresh monthly.

What happens after the three free lessons?

Your free lessons refresh every month, and you can subscribe whenever you want.

What is free, and what does Pro add?

Free gives you 3 AI lessons per month after sign-up, plus the daily mission, spaced review, placement test and mistake bank. Pro — a full year for 60 dollars with coupon KATZU26 — opens every level from A0 to B2 and every situation, with the vocabulary and grammar banks and a daily conversation under a fair-use ceiling.

How do I pay? Is my card stored in the app?

You buy through the official subscription page, and payment works without a bank card. Your activation code arrives within 12 hours at the latest and is redeemed inside the app — we never ask for card details inside the app.

Can I cancel and get my money back?

Yes. Within the first 7 days of your subscription you get a full refund if Katzu does not suit you. After that, paid amounts are not refunded. Details on the refund page.

Which levels are available?

From zero (A0) to B2. If you are a complete beginner that is fine: Katzu explains every correction in Arabic.

Can I type instead of speaking?

Yes. Answer by text or by voice; the microphone is optional. Speaking builds fluency faster, but it is not required to start.

Is my voice stored?

No. It is processed only to understand what you said and is never stored; your mistakes and conversations stay on your device.

What happens to my data?

Your progress and mistakes belong to your account, and you can export your data or delete your account from inside the app at any time. Details on the privacy page.

Is there a fair-use limit?

Yes, a fair daily ceiling that protects the service from abuse. It is announced inside the app before you reach it — not a hidden lock.

Does it work offline?

It is an installable web app (PWA) that stores its content for offline use after the first visit. A live conversation needs an internet connection because the reply comes from the server.

Does Katzu replace a course?

No. You learn the rules in your course, and practise them here.

Does it prepare me for a Goethe or telc exam?

Katzu is an independent training tool, not an official Goethe or telc product, and it issues no certificate or official result. We are currently building exam-format practice (Goethe · telc · DTZ from A1 to B2) and will launch it only when it is ready and measurable.

Is there a native Android app?

The live product is the installable web app (PWA) in your browser — it can be added to your home screen. There is no native Android build published from this repository.
