# 11 — Partnerships (institutional; lower priority — after teachers + groups work)

## Targets (in order)
1. **Private Integrationskurs providers** (Träger): a teacher-facing tool, not a curriculum change. Offer: free trial cohort for one class + the teacher program (doc 06) Model A.
2. **VHS (Volkshochschule) Arabic-speaking course teachers** — as individuals, not the institution (institutions are slow; individual teachers can adopt tools).
3. **Migrant organizations / NGOs** (e.g., city-level Syrian/community associations): free codes for their clients; NO revshare (it would compromise their neutrality) — goodwill + testimonial pipeline instead.

## What to offer (one line each)
- Träger: «أدوات تدريب إضافية لطلابك — بدون تغيير منهجك» ⚠️
- NGO: «أكواد مجانية لمن تخدمهم، تقرير شهري للنتائج (مجهولة الهوية)» ⚠️

## What to avoid
- Any hint of exam-affiliation or BAMF endorsement (orgs are hypersensitive; a rumor here burns the whole channel).
- Free-code dumps without a cohort owner (codes circulate = doc 05 abuse).
- German-language negotiation (owner rule: A2 German → all German-facing contact via a teacher partner acting as interpreter/co-signer).

## Timeline (only after 2+ Lead Partners exist)
Month 3: list 10 orgs via the teachers' own networks (warm intro only — cold institutional email dies). Month 4: one pilot NGO cohort. Kill criterion: if a pilot produces <5 activated learners, institutional is not the channel for this product stage — stop, keep the relationship warm.
