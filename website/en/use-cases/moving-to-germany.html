German practice for moving to Germany

Moving to Germany puts you in situations that need clear, quick German, not only exam German. Katzu practises those situations by voice or text, with Arabic explanations of what the other person is saying.

The six published situations

  • The embassy: an appointment with the clerk — Warum möchten Sie nach Deutschland?
  • The doctor: a German practice — Wo genau tut es weh?
  • A job interview: with a German hiring manager — Erzählen Sie etwas über sich.
  • An apartment viewing: with the landlord — Wann können Sie einziehen?
  • The airport: arrival and entry — Was ist der Zweck Ihrer Reise?
  • The café: ordering in a German café — Ich möchte bitte einen Kaffee.

How to use it for a move

Pick the situation closest to your next appointment, hear the sentence, then speak. Afterwards every mistake returns in four review stops — today, days, week, month — until you say it without thinking.

Clear limits

Katzu is not an immigration office, a lawyer or a legal adviser, and it does not act for you with official bodies. It issues no certificate, visa or work contract. For official decisions use the responsible authority.
