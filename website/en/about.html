About Katzu

Katzu is an Arabic-first German learning app built around speaking, for Arabic speakers who need German in everyday life in Germany. The product is independent; the interface is Arabic-first and German is the practice language.

Who builds Katzu?

Katzu is built by Ghaidak Alloush (ghaidak.com). No ratings, testimonials, user counts or awards are shown here, because we have not collected them and cannot prove them.

How to contact us

For support and questions: support@ghaidak.com. We never ask for a password, a login code or a card number by email.
