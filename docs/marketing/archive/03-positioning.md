# 03 — Positioning

## The wedge (one sentence)
**«تدرّب على امتحان B1 الشفوي بالعربي — قبل ما تدفع ٢٥٩ يورو للامتحان الحقيقي.»** ⚠️
(Gloss: "Rehearse the B1 speaking exam in Arabic — before you pay EUR 259 for the real one." Fee = FACT: EUR 259 Goethe B1 Germany, goethe.de, retrieved 2026-10-01.)

## Positioning statement
For Arabic speakers in Germany who studied German (courses, videos) but still freeze in speaking, **Katzu** is the B1 speaking rehearsal: an exam-style simulation with an honest Arabic debrief and a memory that brings your exact mistakes back until they're gone. Unlike free AI chat or video courses, Katzu trains the one thing the exam tests — you, speaking, under format pressure — alongside (never instead of) your course. [HYPOTHESIS until the side-by-side test, doc 02]

## Messaging house
- **Promise:** امتحانك الشفوي قرب؟ اعرف مستواك الحقيقي قبل يوم الامتحان. ⚠️ (Gloss: "Your speaking exam is near? Know your real level before exam day.")
- **Three proofs:**
  1. Same-shape simulation: three-part speaking practice in the style of B1 exams — plan / present / react. (FACT that the module is authored in this shape; قريباً until loaded.)
  2. Honest Arabic debrief: what you got wrong, why, and the one fix that matters — computed from your own sentences, not flattery. (FACT: deterministic debrief exists.)
  3. Memory: your mistakes return in spaced review until mastered. (FACT: SRS shipped.)
- **Reasons to believe:** teacher partners review content; founder appears on camera; no affiliation claims; free first mock with no payment data.
