# 06 — Teacher program

## Who to target (criteria, in priority order)
1. Arabic-speaking German teachers with 100–5,000 learners in their orbit (Telegram channel / WhatsApp groups / small institute). Big enough to matter, small enough to answer a DM.
2. Already sell something (own course, V-IZ affiliation, exam-prep sessions) → revshare is familiar, not exotic.
3. Active in the last 30 days (posts, replies). Dead channels waste trial codes.
4. Willing to put their name on it (the disclosure rule below selects honest partners automatically).
5. No invented names: build the 15-teacher list from the owner's own contacts + groups observed in doc 08 recon. The owner said they have community and teacher access (FACT).

## 15-teacher scoring rubric (1–5 each; approach top 8 first)
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Learner orbit size & activity | ×3 |
| B1-exam relevance of their teaching (not general A1) | ×3 |
| Existing paid offer (familiarity with revshare) | ×2 |
| Responsiveness to DMs | ×2 |
| Willingness to review content (German QC layer) | ×2 |
| Overlap risk (already sells a competing mock product) | −(×2) |

## Terms (one model per teacher — never both, ambiguity kills trust)
- **Model A — Wholesale:** teacher buys ≥10 codes at EUR 15–18 (TEST TARGET), sells at public price, keeps the margin. They own their price risk; good for institutes.
- **Model B — Revshare:** teacher shares trial codes free; earns 50% of net on verified paid conversions from their tracked cohort (≈EUR 9–11 per sale at the 29 cell — see 05 table). Paid monthly via Wise/bank with a per-code statement. Disclosed to buyers (wording below). Good for individual teachers/creators.
- **Trial codes:** 14-day, single-use, expiring, personal DM only, tracked per teacher for attribution.
- **Payout timing:** monthly, on verified paid orders only (order paid + first session completed + no refund pending). No payout on refunds.
- **Content-review duty (the genius clause):** teacher reviews the B1 module's German/Arabic before their cohort starts. Aligns incentives: they won't stamp their name on bad content, and the owner's A2 German never faces a learner alone.

## Tiers
| Tier | Requirement | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Partner | rubric pass + first trial cohort run | trial codes, 50% revshare, onboarding kit |
| Lead Partner | ≥10 paid conversions in 90 days | +EUR 2/code on wholesale OR 55% revshare; featured in their city's group posts as "المدرّب المعتمد" ⚠️ (careful: "معتمد" only means Katzu-verified partner — wording must not imply exam authority; native read + legal flag) |
| Institute | ≥50 codes/quarter | custom cohort reports, co-branded challenge (doc 08) |

## Buyer-facing disclosure wording (mandatory, on checkout near teacher-attributed codes) ⚠️
«هذا الكود من شراكة مع الأستاذ/ة [الاسم]، ويحصل على عمولة عند اشتراكك — سعره لك نفسه.» (Gloss: "This code is a partnership with Teacher X, who earns a commission on your purchase — the price is the same for you.")

## Onboarding kit (what the teacher receives)
1. 1-page Arabic handout (doc 07 §handout).
2. 10–30 trial codes with their attribution tag.
3. The 45-min class-demo plan (doc 07).
4. The 30-sec demo video (doc 09 top variant).
5. Dashboard-lite numbers (owner reads from admin views until an engineering run adds a teacher portal — SPEC only, doc 19): codes shared, activations, mock completions, paid conversions, their payout estimate.
6. These terms, in Arabic, one page.

## Red flags for bad partners
- Asks for bulk free codes "for friends" without a cohort plan.
- Resells trial codes (trial = free; selling a free code = program violation).
- Refuses the buyer-disclosure line.
- Promises students a pass guarantee (banned claim — doc 04) → one warning, then revoke.
- Buries Katzu in spam blasts → damages the brand in exactly the trust-based groups we need; cobra-effect guard from 05.
