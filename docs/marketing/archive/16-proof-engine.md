# 16 — Proof engine (10 voice-note stories in 90 days)

North star context: mocks completed before exam date + score change. Proof = honest artifacts only; survivorship guard applies (report misses too, §rules).

## Pipeline (who becomes a story)
1. Everyone who sets an exam date (in-app prompt, doc 15 E1) enters the pipeline sheet: learner · exam date · baseline mock score · consent status.
2. Trigger moments to ask: (a) score improvement ≥15 points between mocks; (b) 7 days before their exam (the countdown message, doc 17, includes the ask); (c) post-exam reply either way.
3. Weekly sweep (15 min, part of the Sunday batch, doc 09): pick ≤3 asks — never mass-ask.

## Voice-note request script (WhatsApp) ⚠️
> يا هلا {اسم}! شفت إن تقدمك بالمحاكيات وصل {التقدم} — مبروز مقدماً 🌟
> بدي منك خدمة صغيرة إذا بتحب: تسجيل صوتي بـ٩٠ ثانية تحكي فيه بجملتك: شو كنت خايف منه قبل كَاتْزُو، شو سويت، وشي صار.
> بستخدمه بمقطع قصير + بصوتك فقط، وبقلك قبل النشر شو نوا. إذا ما حابب، عادي تماماً — وما بيتأثر أي شي عليك.
(Gloss: recognition of real progress; what's asked; 90 seconds; their voice; approval before publish; no pressure.)

## Consent wording (must be explicit, written, before any use) ⚠️
> «بموافقتي: بصوتهم وصورتي إِن وجدت في محتوى كَاتْزُو (تسويق وموقع)، وأقدر أتراجع بأي وقت برسالة، والسحب بينشر باسمي الأول بس. — {الاسم}، {التاريخ}»
Store: consent text + screenshot of their affirmative reply, in the private sheet (never in the repo).

## Case-study template (each story, one row)
learner alias · city · level · exam date · baseline score · final mock score · mock count · the one thing that changed (their words) · exam outcome (if known) · consent ref · published where.
Honesty rules: real alias or first name + city only; if the exam outcome is unknown, say «امتحانه قريب» — never imply a pass; publish improvement numbers as mock scores with the «تدريبي تقريبي» qualifier (doc 15 F1 wording). If a consenting learner FAILED, we still may publish (with their blessing) the improvement framing — survivorship guard: the proof engine is allowed to show «تدريب ما كفى لوحده» lessons; hiding failures would poison the brand's one differentiator (honesty).

## Shareable score card (in-app generated image; spec only, engineering later)
- Header: «بطاقة تقدّمي — كَاتْزُو»
- Body: baseline score → current score, mock count, days to exam, one strength line.
- Footer: «نتيجة محاكاة تدريبية — ليست نتيجة امتحان رسمية» (mandatory) + {APP_URL}.
- Caption template for sharing ⚠️: «من {قبل} إلى {بعد} بمحاكيات كَاتْزُو — التدريب بيفرق. جرّب انت المحاكاة المجانية:»
- No leaderboard of absolute scores anywhere (privilege fairness across levels; improvement only).

## Distribution
Landing proof section (doc 12) when ≥3 stories exist; V8 video (doc 09) unlocks at the first honest card; community cross-post with the learner's own share (never ours alone — their voice, their post, our support).
