How Katzu works

From a real situation to a sentence you say yourself: study the phrases, check your understanding, speak or type, then review what needs fixing. Not a list of words without context.

  1. Choose a situation

    Six situations from life in Germany: the embassy, the doctor, the job interview, the apartment viewing, the airport and the café. Each one starts at your level.

  2. Hear it, translate when stuck

    Tap the speaker to hear the correct pronunciation; the Arabic translation sits under the line and only matters when you need it.

  3. Speak or type

    Record your German sentence or type it. The microphone is optional, and the correction comes back in Arabic immediately — a mistake is explained, never mocked.

  4. Get a correction with a reason

    Katzu gives you the correct form and why: zum Arzt not nach Arzt, and age uses sein, not haben.

  5. Every mistake comes back

    One mistake, four review stops: today, after days, after a week, after a month. Say it correctly at the last stop and it is yours.

  6. Keep the habit

    A short daily mission stays open for free — review and the mistake bank are never locked behind a subscription.

What Katzu is not

Not an official exam course, no certificate or official grade, not a replacement for a lawyer, doctor or immigration adviser. Exam-format practice (Goethe · telc · DTZ, A1–B2) ships only when ready and measurable.
