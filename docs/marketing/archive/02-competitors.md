## Teardown

### 1. The real competitor: free status quo (Integrationskurs + YouTube/Telegram teachers)
- **Promise:** state-funded German to B1; Arabic YouTube/Telegram teachers explain grammar and phrases free. (FACT: course funding scale — ~363,466 Integrationskurs starters 2024, Mediendienst-Integration summarizing BAMF, https://mediendienst-integration.de/bildung/sprache/wie-viele-personen-nehmen-an-integrationskursen-teil/, retrieved 2026-10-01.)
- **Price:** free to the learner.
- **Strength:** zero price, trusted institutions/teachers, cohort belonging. Status-quo bias is the strongest force against any purchase.
- **Weakness:** 20+ learners/class → minutes of speaking per person per day; no per-learner mistake memory; exam-specific speaking rehearsal rare; teachers are generalists, not exam-format specialists.
- **Katzu user's line:** «الكورس بيقوّيني، بس ما بحكيش غير دقيقتين باليوم. كاتزو مكان التمرين اللي بيخلي الكلام يطلع.» ⚠️ (Gloss: "The course builds me, but I only speak two minutes a day. Katzu is the practice that makes the words come out.")

### 3. V-IZ — Arabic-facing B1 video course
- **Price:** EUR 149.99 course + EUR 46 books (owner research, FACT baseline; site not fetched — mark for verification).
- **Promise:** complete B1 preparation in Arabic with mock exams and AI speaking feedback.
- **Strength:** structured curriculum, Arabic-first teaching, exam-focused, established trust.
- **Weakness:** video = passive; speaking practice is bolted on; one-time price is high for the allowance segment; no daily habit loop.
- **Katzu user's line:** «شاهدت الكورس؟ حلو. بس الامتحان حكي مش مشاهدة.» ⚠️ (Gloss: "Watched the course? Nice. But the exam is speaking, not watching.")

### 4. Preply/italki-style human tutors
- **Price:** roughly EUR 10–30/hour (market observation — ASSUMPTION, verify on preply.com before quoting).
- **Strength:** real human, real conversation.
- **Weakness:** EUR 150–300 for a prep course (owner research FACT) or recurring hourly cost; scheduling; variable quality; no systematic mistake memory.
- **Katzu user's line:** «المعلم بالساعة، وكاتزو بالدقيقة — متى ما حبيت.» ⚠️

### 5. Babbel / Duolingo
- **Strength:** habit design, polish, brand.
- **Weakness (verify on their pages):** no Arabic support language for German; Duolingo German does not target B1 speaking-exam format; Babbel subscription ~USD 17.95/mo retail (PCMag, https://uk.pcmag.com/education/1160/babbel, retrieved 2026-10-01).
- **Katzu user's line:** «بالعربي، لامتحانك، وبصوتك.» ⚠️

### 6. ChatGPT voice / issen (general AI tutors)
- **Strength:** free/cheap, fluent, available now.
- **Weakness:** sycophantic ("gut gemacht!" to broken German), no exam structure, no level caps, no Arabic-native error taxonomy, no memory discipline, map≠territory: talking to an LLM ≠ exam format.
- **This is the most dangerous comparison because it's free.** Answer with the side-by-side test below — not with words.

### 7. Ankommen app (government app for arrivals)
- **Strength:** official, free, German-government trust.
- **Weakness:** information/orientation app, not a speaking rehearsal; no personalized correction loop (verify current version).
- **Katzu user's line:** «انكنّ للمعرفة، كاتزو للتدريب.» ⚠️

## The 10-learner side-by-side test (Katzu vs ChatGPT voice) — run AFTER the exam module loads
**Why:** the only honest answer to "ChatGPT is free" is a repeatable demonstration. Hypothesis: on exam-shaped tasks Katzu's honest debrief + format fidelity beats a raw LLM visibly.

**Protocol:**
1. Recruit 10 learners at B1-ish level (beta cohort). Each does BOTH: (a) one Katzu B1-style mock, (b) one 15-min ChatGPT voice session with the prompt "اجعلني أتدرب على امتحان B1 شفوي".
2. Randomize order. Same week. No coaching.
3. Score each experience 1–5 on: exam-format match; correction honesty (did it flag real errors?); error explanation in Arabic; memory ("will it remember my mistakes next time?" — no=1/yes=5); would-recommend.
4. Learner ranks: which better prepared you for exam day? (forced choice)
5. Pass: ≥7/10 rank Katzu first AND mean honesty score ≥4 for Katzu vs ChatGPT's. Fail: publish nothing about the comparison; revisit positioning.
- Output: an internal scoring sheet + (with written consent) quotable verdicts for doc 16.
