# 10 — Lead magnets & SEO (zero-budget; no SEO pages built now)

## Lead magnets (original, cheap to make, exam-intent)
1. **«فحص جاهزيتك للـ B1 الشفوي» — 12-question Arabic self-check** (Google Form / Telegram bot later): questions probe the three exam parts, habits, exam date. Output: honest readiness estimate + the free-mock link. This is the exam-date capture tool (commitment lever + doc 16 pipeline). Build week 2.
2. **«أخطاء الـ B1 العشرة الكبيرة» PDF** — 10 original error pairs (Arabic explanation, German examples) drawn from the app's own correction style. Ends each page with the free-mock CTA. Teacher-reviewed German. Build week 3.
3. **«سيناريو امتحان كامل بالصوت»** — one original part-2 topic with the owner's model answer audio + the debrief read out. Telegram-channel exclusive (drives channel joins). Build week 4.

## PWA/ASO basics (the PWA is the store page today)
- The landing IS the listing: title, description, screenshots per MARKETING-KIT §6 checklist (real content, Arabic UI, no personal data).
- Install prompt (doc 15 string spec) fires after the free mock — after value, not before.
- When a Play Store wrapper eventually ships: ASO title «كاتزو — تعلم الألمانية بالتحدث», screenshots per the same checklist; no keyword stuffing, no competitor names.
- pages.dev URL is temporary (MARKETING-KIT §8 rule) — campaigns wait for the final domain. OWNER dependency.

## What NOT to build now (explicit cuts)
- No programmatic SEO pages before real funnel data exists (doc 19): content without distribution rots.
- No English/German landing variant: launch market is Arabic-speaking; German-facing material goes through teacher partners only.
