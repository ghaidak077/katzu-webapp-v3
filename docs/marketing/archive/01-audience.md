## Persona cards

### P1 — أمير، "الورقة": benefit recipient preparing B1 to secure residence [HYPOTHESIS]
- **Situation:** 25–40, arrived 2022–2024, on Bürgergeld/allowance, B1 certificate = residence security (§ residenzpflicht context — verify per case). Months into an Integrationskurs or waiting for a spot.
- **JTBD:** pass B1 Sprechen without freezing; the certificate is the door to work and safety.
- **Fears:** failing = waiting months + re-fee; being humiliated in front of the Prüfer; "my Arabic accent makes German harder".
- **Objections:** no card, no spare money; "free YouTube exists"; "the course already prepares me".
- **Trust signals:** teacher endorsement, Arabic UI, visible non-affiliation honesty, another Syrian/Iraqi learner's voice note.
- **Payment ability:** EUR 19–29 one-time possible if framed against the EUR 259 exam fee (mental accounting); instalments unlikely without infrastructure. No card → manual transfer/code.
- **Where he is:** WhatsApp family/community groups, Telegram Arabic-German groups, TikTok, mosque/cultural centers offline.
- **Trigger moment to buy:** exam date booked (the letter/email from the exam center) — 4–6 weeks out.

### P2 — ليلى, "الترقية": worker wanting a better job [HYPOTHESIS]
- **Situation:** 27–45, employed below qualification (cleaning, delivery, care), B1/B2 needed for Ausbildung or better work.
- **JTBD:** speak confidently in work situations; B1 certificate unlocks Ausbildung.
- **Fears:** time poverty (shifts); wasting money on another course that doesn't move the needle.
- **Objections:** "I don't have time"; "apps are for beginners"; "what's different from V-IZ?"
- **Trust signals:** concrete scenario list (job interview, Ausbildung), sample debrief, price transparency.
- **Payment ability:** best of all personas — has income, card possible; also responds to employer/FH payout? No — solo buyer.
- **Where:** LinkedIn rarely; WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram.
- **Trigger:** rejected from a job/course for "insufficient German", or an Ausbildung deadline.

### P3 — يوسف, "الطالب": student [HYPOTHESIS]
- **Situation:** 18–26, Studienkolleg/university applicant, needs B1/B2 for admission; tech-native.
- **JTBD:** efficient prep that fits around classes; hates wasting time.
- **Objections:** "ChatGPT does this free"; "why not Lisan (free)?"
- **Trust signals:** the honest side-by-side vs ChatGPT (doc 02), score mechanics, mock format fidelity.
- **Payment ability:** low but exists (family support); student price sensitivity high → the €19 cell likely wins here.
- **Where:** Telegram, Reddit r/DuolingoGerman? no — r/German, TikTok, Discord study groups.
- **Trigger:** admission letter with a language deadline.

### P4 — أم سامر, "الوسيط": family member helping a parent [HYPOTHESIS]
- **Situation:** 20–35, joined family reunification, own German better, manages the household's paperwork; the parent must pass A2/B1.
- **JTBD:** find a tool the parent will actually use alone, in Arabic, without hand-holding.
- **Objections:** "will my mother manage the app?"; "is it a scam?" (she pays).
- **Trust signals:** the founder's face (on camera), clear refund terms, code-based gifting ("buy a code for your father").
- **Payment ability:** moderate; often the actual payer for P1-type learners.
- **Where:** Facebook groups (strongest for women), WhatsApp.
- **Trigger:** parent's exam letter; family reunification language requirement news.

## Moment map (first hearing → payment) [HYPOTHESIS]
1. **Hearing:** teacher's WhatsApp message / group post / TikTok video (doc 07/08/09).
2. **Skepticism (the real gate):** "scam? official? another app?" — resolved by: non-affiliation honesty, free mock with no payment data, teacher's name on it.
3. **First value:** free mock completed in ≤10 min, honest Arabic debrief (zero-price effect; Fogg: ability high).
4. **Score shock:** "my mock says 42/100" — loss aversion activates.
5. **Commitment:** enters exam date at signup (commitment & consistency); readiness % shows the gap (goal gradient).
6. **Pay moment:** anchored vs EUR 259 + 3-month wait; teacher's 50%-off code or direct EUR 29 (TEST TARGET); manual transfer OR hosted checkout (doc 13).
7. **Post-purchase:** activation code arrives fast (trust confirm), day-1 first session (doc 17).
