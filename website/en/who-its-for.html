Who Katzu is for — and who it is not for

We do not sell it to everyone. An honest comparison: if you are in the first list it will help you, and if you are in the second we will say so.

It fits you if…

  • You are an Arabic speaker moving to Germany for work, study or family life, and you need to speak rather than only memorise.
  • You have just arrived and face situations like the embassy, a doctor, work or an apartment, and want to rehearse first.
  • Your level is between A0 and B2, and you prefer Arabic explanations to a course delivered entirely in German.
  • You want a short daily conversation by voice or text, with direct correction and four review stops per mistake.
  • You want to know what you get before paying: 3 free lessons refreshed every month, a yearly Pro price in plain numbers, and a 7-day refund.

It may not fit you if…

  • You want a comprehensive grammar textbook or a long academic curriculum; Katzu starts from situations.
  • You want an official certificate or a guaranteed pass in Goethe, telc or DTZ; none is issued.
  • You need legal, medical or immigration advice; use a licensed professional.
  • You want a human tutor or a social network; Katzu is an individual practice tool.
  • You only want flashcards without conversation; review exists, but producing German is the core.

What is actually supported now

Six situations from life in Germany — embassy, doctor, job interview, apartment viewing, airport, café — at levels A0–B2, by voice or text. Anything else is unpublished and not promised here.
