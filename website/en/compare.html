Katzu compared with the alternatives

We do not claim Katzu is better than everything. Each tool has a different job; this is a comparison against what we can actually say about our own product.

Against flashcard apps

Flashcards are excellent for quick recall, but they often test whether you recognise a word. Katzu asks you to produce German in a situation, by voice or text, then corrects you and turns the mistake into a review stop.

Against a general course

A general course builds broad, sequenced progress. Katzu starts from the situations that follow a move to Germany and explains in Arabic. If you want comprehensive grammar, a general course fits better.

Against a human tutor

A tutor offers a relationship and human judgement no app can copy. Katzu offers practice at any time for one yearly price, but it is not a replacement for a tutor and issues no certificate.

Against exam-prep courses

Official exam courses train the official format. Katzu builds the A0–B2 foundation through conversation, and exam-format practice is being built — measurable before published, never promised early.

What we do not claim

No ratings, testimonials, user counts or awards. No guaranteed exam pass or job offer. Only what can be checked in the app and its policies.
